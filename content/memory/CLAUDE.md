## What Lives Here

The durable knowledge layer — a wiki of markdown pages the assistant writes and
maintains. Pages start as flat siblings of this file, catalogued below by category;
once a theme grows enough to warrant it, move it into a sub-folder with its own
`CLAUDE.md` navigation node and register that folder under Deep-Dive Navigation.

This file is the navigation hub. The `wiki` and `task-management` skills read it to
find what exists, so **every new page must be listed here** — a page missing from
the catalog is invisible to them.

It's empty on purpose. Fill it by dropping documents into `content/sources/` and
asking the assistant to ingest them, or by asking it to write up what you know.

## Suggested shape

Some folders worth creating once you have material for them:

| Folder | For |
|---|---|
| `my-team/` | People you manage: 1:1 context, career, performance |
| `collaborators/` | Peers, stakeholders, teams you advise |
| `concepts/` | Reusable frameworks and playbooks |
| `project-management/` | Planning surface, board snapshots |
| `roadmaps/` | Quarterly or time-boxed plans |

Anything about a person belongs on the relevant project or team page — the `wiki`
skill deliberately never creates standalone person pages.
